Reward proof, not confident prose

R = clamp(C − 0.20 F − 0.10 U, 0, 1)

C: supported target coverage. F: distinct false findings. U: distinct unsupported findings.

A cloth match cannot identify a killer. Paper ownership cannot establish forgery. A correct accusation without acquired support scores zero.

Comparisons can compose into proof. Narrative uncertainty is retained separately; narrative itself is not automatically fact-checked.
