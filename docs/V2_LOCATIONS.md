# Locations — current canonical world

Author-only review. This document contains private information and is excluded from the detective image and command workspace.

## Mercy House archive (archive)

The lamps have been turned up too high. Above their yellow circles the archive disappears into beams and soot, and each time the wind presses against the casement the flames lean together. A desk stands under the window, its blotter crowded by ordinary papers. Beyond it are shelves, a cold fireplace and a service door whose lower edge has worn a pale arc into the boards. From the scullery comes the intermittent complaint of a kettle. The narrow opening catches a line of light from the adjoining kitchen.

Visible routes: hall, scullery. Searchable features: desk, fireplace, floor, shelves.

## Mercy House entrance hall (hall)

Meltwater drops steadily from the coats along the wall. The entrance hall smells of damp wool, lamp oil and the polish used on the visitors' book stand. One passage leads back to the archive, another to the scullery; the courtyard door knocks softly against its latch. Farther along, the ninth ward remains bolted. There is room for two people to pass comfortably here, provided neither chooses to stand in the way.

Visible routes: archive, scullery, courtyard, ward. Searchable features: visitors book, coat stand.

## Mercy House scullery (scullery)

The scullery is warmer than the rest of the house, but the warmth ends at the floor. A range holds a kettle and a blackened pan; a wash basin waits beneath the window. Beside the range, the service hatch opens onto a sliver of the archive desk. Standing at it, you can see how a person might notice a sleeve or a moving hand and miss the face above it. Doors return to the archive and entrance hall.

Visible routes: archive, hall. Searchable features: range, wash basin, service hatch.

## Mercy House courtyard (courtyard)

Snow collects along the courtyard walls without yet settling in the wheel ruts. A gate lamp turns the falling flakes into brief bright scratches. Behind you Mercy House has too many dark windows; ahead, the road drops toward the square. A narrower way runs beside the wall to Dev's winter garden. A kitchen clang carries into the courtyard and is gone almost before you turn toward it.

Visible routes: hall, square, garden. Searchable features: gate, cart tracks.

## Town square (square)

The square has emptied before the shops have quite closed. Shutters answer one another across the road, and a horse drinks noisily at the trough beneath the notice board. The Pine Lantern keeps a strip of light under its door. Paths lead toward the chapel, the Das and Rao households, and the Mercy House courtyard; beyond the last lamp the forest road becomes a different shade of black. You remember this crossing from childhood, though the signs above the shops have changed.

Visible routes: courtyard, das_house, rao_house, inn, chapel, forest. Searchable features: notice board, horse trough.

## Dev’s house and winter garden (garden)

Dev's house shelters behind winter beds edged with salvaged slate. Snow has softened most of the garden into one surface, broken by the tool shed and the kitchen table visible under a lean-to. Somewhere under the beds are last month's bulbs; from where you stand you cannot tell whether anything more recent lies beneath them. A path follows the fence toward the cemetery, while the courtyard lamp remains visible behind you.

Visible routes: courtyard, cemetery. Searchable features: winter beds, tool shed, kitchen table.

## Das household (das_house)

The Das parlor is narrow enough that a visitor must turn sideways to let someone pass. Tea has dried in a saucer on the parlor table. Beside the stove stands an ash bucket; across the room, a writing desk has been closed and locked. Everything has the cared-for appearance of objects kept through more winters than their owners expected. The only street door returns to the square.

Visible routes: square. Searchable features: stove, writing desk, parlor table.

## Rao household (rao_house)

The threshold has been lowered and the study furniture set far apart, leaving a clear passage for a wheelchair. On the workbench, acoustic drawings lie under smooth stones. A correspondence drawer sits closed beneath them. From the courtyard comes the click of a loose gate fastening; the street beyond returns to the square. The wheels have left two parallel tracks in the rug beside the workbench.

Visible routes: square. Searchable features: workbench, correspondence drawer.

## Chapel (chapel)

The chapel has kept the day's cold. A few candles burn near the inner door, too small to warm the stone, and a register lies on the keeper's side desk. Pews make quiet islands of darkness beneath the windows. People sometimes come here because they want witnesses, and sometimes because they do not. The square is behind the outer door; a side path crosses to the cemetery.

Visible routes: square, cemetery. Searchable features: register desk, pews.

## Old cemetery (cemetery)

The oldest stones lean in different directions, as though the ground has shifted separately beneath each family. Beyond the named graves, fir needles lie over smaller unmarked mounds. The burial office is shut against the weather. Paths lead to the chapel, the winter garden and the forest edge. A line of fir needles has gathered along the burial-office step.

Visible routes: garden, chapel, forest. Searchable features: unnamed graves, burial office.

## Black Fir Forest edge (forest)

At the forest edge the last house-light vanishes as soon as you turn your head. Fir branches move above an otherwise sheltered path. Someone has cut marks into the milestone, but weather has made them difficult to read. The wind brings the resinous smell of freshly broken branches. The paths return to the square and cemetery or climb toward the old quarry.

Visible routes: square, cemetery, quarry. Searchable features: path, milestone.

## Old stone quarry (quarry)

The quarry opens abruptly beyond the firs. Water has frozen along the exposed cuts, and a broken cart rail points into empty air. A survey hut leans beneath the bank where the wind cannot quite reach it. The forest path is your way back. A loose stone travels down the cut face, strikes twice and falls silent.

Visible routes: forest. Searchable features: cart rail, survey hut.

## Sealed ninth ward (ward)

Inside the ninth ward the air has the stillness of a cupboard left closed for years. Bare bed frames stand under washed-out rectangles where notices once hung. A glass-fronted key box catches your lamp; a narrow recess interrupts the plaster near the far wall. Dust has collected against the inside of the door, interrupted now by the line of its opening.

Visible routes: hall. Searchable features: key box, bed frames, wall recess.

## Pine Lantern inn (inn)

The Pine Lantern's dining room has divided itself into people waiting for news and people pretending to finish supper. A lodging book rests behind the counter. Wet boots have made dark half-moons around the stove. The road outside leads back into the square, where the coach will stop at dawn if the snow allows it.

Visible routes: square. Searchable features: counter, lodging book.
